# Educator guide

Objectives: identify producers and consumers, connect light/water/plants to survival, trace food energy, and predict effects of one changed condition. Suggested session: 10–15 minutes. Ask learners to explain arrows as energy from food to eater.

Model assumptions: plant capacity is round((light+water)/2), herbivore capacity is round((plant+water)/3) when food exists, predator capacity is round((herbivore+water)/3); values clamp 0–10. These are model values, not measurements. Sources: National Geographic Food Chain (https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/food-chain/); National Park Service Food Webs (https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/food-webs.htm). Offline follow-up: draw a local food chain and circle one resource that could change.
