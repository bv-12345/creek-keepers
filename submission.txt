The app includes habitat construction (two producers, two herbivores, predator and labelled energy arrows), three light/water prediction scenarios with deterministic one-step model, and unfamiliar dry-creek restoration. Model values are bounded 0–10 and explained in EDUCATOR_GUIDE.md. Test report records the actual Node model check, local HTTP 200 check, responsive/keyboard review, and limitations. No login, wallet, personal data, tracking, external runtime service, or child testing.
